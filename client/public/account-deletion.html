Delete your Omni account

Last updated: 29 May 2026

You can delete your Omni account and all associated personal data at any time, from inside the app or by emailing us. This page explains how — exactly as required by the Google Play Account Deletion policy.

1. From inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open Omni on your phone and sign in with the phone number on the account.
  2. Tap the Settings icon (top right of the home screen).
  3. Scroll to the bottom — Delete Account.
  4. Type DELETE in the confirmation field.
  5. Tap Delete My Account.

Open Settings →

2. From the website

  1. Go to the sign-in page and sign in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Follow the same steps as above.

3. By email (if you cannot sign in)

Email support@omni.app from the phone number's registered email (if any), or include your phone number and the last 4 characters of your most recent OTP request. We may ask one additional verification question to make sure you own the account. Requests are honoured within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (name, phone number, language, preferences).
  • All your grocery lists, items, pantry records, and meal plans.
  • Your transactions, bills, and subscription records.
  • Your assistant chat history.
  • Your push-notification tokens (you will stop receiving alerts immediately).
  • All active sessions; you will be signed out on every device.
  • Your affiliate click logs and other behavioural data tied to your account.

What may remain

  • Items you added to a grocery list owned by another family member may stay in that shared list — their list, their data.
  • Aggregated, anonymised statistics (e.g. "how many bills were scanned in May"); these cannot be linked back to you.
  • Records we are legally required to retain (e.g. limited tax-related data, fraud-prevention logs) for the minimum period required by law.
How long does deletion take? Immediate sign-out and removal from active systems happens within minutes. Full purge from backups and replicated systems completes within 30 days.

Need help or have questions?

Email support@omni.app. We respond personally to every deletion email.

See our Privacy Policy for the full details of what we collect, why, and how long we keep it.

Deleting your account is final. We do not retain a copy you can restore. If you simply want to take a break, you can sign out and your data will be waiting when you return.
